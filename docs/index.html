Each hand is played by the real game engine (the same rules engine EDOPro uses), up to the end of your first turn, against an opponent who really holds and uses the hand traps you tick. The score is how many interruptions your end board has, plus 0.5 per different kind of interruption. Lines that score well are saved and replayed in later runs, so the lab gets better at finding combos over time. Every report starts with a health check (crashes, missing hands) and the calibration check (does the lab still reach the guide's own tested boards).

Draft for Belal to review. Nothing goes into the guide until he approves it.

## 1. Medius + 4 non-engine cards (file m1a)
1. Normal Summon **Medius the Pure** → Special Summon **Nervedo** from the Deck.
2. Nervedo: banish the top 3 face-down → destroy it → **Nerva** (put it in M5).
   - *Bridge:* Nervedo goes to the Extra Deck face-up → its trigger Special Summons **Junoldo** from the Deck.
3. Junoldo: banish the top 3 → **Junora** (Synchro).
   - *Bridge:* Junoldo goes to the Extra Deck face-up → add **Nervedo back from the Extra Deck to your hand**. This is the Lv7+ Artmage that Non-Finito needs later.
4. Link **Medius + Junora** → **Purification Power Patron** (left Extra Monster Zone). Junora is a Power Patron in the GY, so you get the search: **Theorealize Medius**.
5. **Theorealize Medius** Special Summons itself (a Power Patron card is on the field) → add **Theorealize Past Lull**.
6. **Non-Finito**: discard Past Lull + Tribute Nervedo from your hand → Set **Impasto**.
7. **Past Lull from the GY** (its 2nd effect): banish it → revive **Junora** into M2, the zone Purification points to.
8. **Medius from the GY**: shuffle Theorealize Medius into the Deck → Special Summon Medius.
   - *Why:* Non-Finito's opponent's-turn Fusion needs Medius on the field.
9. Set **Infinite Impermanence** if you have it. From the hand it only works while you control no cards.

**End board:** Nerva, Non-Finito, Junora, Medius, Purification Power Patron, Impasto (+ Imperm Set) + the rest of your hand.

**On their turn:**
- **Non-Finito + Nerva = board wipe.** Non-Finito's quick Fusion (Medius + Non-Finito → Diactorus) is an Artmage monster effect, so Nerva turns it into "destroy all cards your opponent controls". Wait until they have committed 3+ cards.
- **Impasto:** negate + destroy a monster effect. You have 3 Types (Illusion, Fairy, Fiend), so it also bounces all their Spells/Traps.
- **Purification:** quick-Link with Medius → **S:P Little Knight** (banish 1) or **Medicurius**.
- **Risk:** the 6 face-down banishes can hit 1-ofs. If Junoldo is hit, Nervedo's trigger Special Summons Theorealize Medius instead.

## 2. Black Chaos + Medius (file bc1): steps 1–8 above, then the ritual wing
9. Discard **Black Chaos** → place **Mind Shuffle** face-up from the Deck.
10. **Mind Shuffle:** add **Skull Archfiend**, then discard Skull Archfiend.
    - *Bridge:* Skull sent to the GY → send **LDR** from the Deck to the GY → add **Magician of Dark Chaos**.
11. **Skull Archfiend from the GY:** put Black Chaos + Nervedo + Past Lull on the bottom → Special Summon Skull. **LDR stays in the GY** for later turns.

**End board:** Nerva, Non-Finito, Junora, Medius, Skull Archfiend, Purification Power Patron, Impasto, Mind Shuffle (face-up), Magician of Dark Chaos in hand.

**On their turn:** everything from line 1, plus **Mind Shuffle**: when they activate, return Junora (Lv10) → Special Summon **Magician of Dark Chaos** ignoring conditions → banish 1 of their cards face-down + add a Spell from your GY.

## 3. Engraver + Medius (file e3)
1. Discard **Engraver** → add **Tract**.
2. **Tract** → add **Lacrima**, discard Lacrima.
3. **Engraver from the GY:** shuffle Lacrima back → Special Summon Engraver.
4. Link **Engraver** → **Requiem**.
5. Tribute Requiem → **Lacrima from the Deck** → Lacrima sends **Engraver #2** to the GY.
6. The Medius line (steps 1–7 of line 1). **Purification uses Lacrima + Junora**, so Medius stays on the field and no Medius revive is needed.

**End board:** Nerva, Non-Finito, Junora, Medius, Theorealize Medius, Purification Power Patron, Impasto.
- In the GY: Lacrima (on their turn, revives Requiem), Tract, Engraver.

## 4. Ars Magna of Infinity and Finity + 4 non-engine cards (file inf1)
1. Banish **Infinity** from your hand → add **Ars Magna P&C**.
2. Banish P&C from your hand → add **Citrinitas**.
3. Activate **Citrinitas** → add **Medius**.
4. Normal Summon Medius → **Nervedo** → **Nerva**.
   - *Bridge:* Nerva is a Fusion → **Infinity comes back** from banishment.
   - Nervedo's Extra Deck trigger → **Finmel** from the Deck.
5. Link **Medius + Infinity** → **Purification Power Patron**.
   - *Bridge:* a Link was Summoned → **P&C comes back** from banishment.

**End board:** Nerva, Finmel, Ars Magna P&C, Purification Power Patron, Citrinitas.

**On their turn:**
- **Finmel + Nerva = wipe.** Finmel's quick negate is an Artmage effect (4 Types on the field).
- **P&C:** banish their Spells/Traps.
- **Purification:** quick-Link.
- **Citrinitas:** lets the Ars Magna effects be used as Quick Effects.
